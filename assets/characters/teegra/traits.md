# Teegra — curator notes (VLM-assisted, film stills)

Identity anchor for the Fire & Ice (1983) remake heroine.

- Skin: pale, fair, cool undertone.
- Hair: very long jet-black, parted down the middle, loose.
- Eyes: green.
- Makeup: winged eyeliner, muted red lips.
- Build: slender, athletic (barbarian-princess).
- Costume: lavender inverted-triangle gathered-center halter top (see teegra.top.halter).
- Prop: obsidian dagger carried at the hip (see props/melee.obsidian_dagger).

Colour rule: VLM-reported colours outrank any hand-typed name below.
