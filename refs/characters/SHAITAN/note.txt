What Shaitan is
---------------
Shaitan is Teegra's companion at Fire Keep (beat-sheet S05: "Teegra, Tutor, Shaitan (pet)";
roster: appearance "TBD"). It has exactly one line addressed to it in the whole film —
L048, spoken by Teegra in shot 00196: "Don't we, Shaitan?"

The film's own frames answer this: Shaitan is a BLACK FELINE (big-cat scale, panther-like),
not a human. It is in the chamber during the lesson scene.

Evidence (all from S05, the Fire Keep study, shots 00190 and 00196)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
- shot 00190 f00069 — almost the whole animal visible: lying on a deep red cushion, head
  turned slightly toward camera, front paws tucked, hindquarters and most of the tail in
  frame, nothing overlapping it. Clean silhouette. BEST CANDIDATE.
- shot 00190 f00138 — same animal, sphinx pose on the red cushion; hindquarters cut off by
  the crop; a human hand rests on the same cushion but does not overlap the body.
- shot 00196 f00000 — head + one eye only, bottom-right corner, right beside Teegra's face
  (this is the shot where she says "Don't we, Shaitan?"). Too tightly cropped for a ref,
  but it is the strongest link between the animal and the name.

Reported appearance (local vision model, two independent passes agreed on the animal):
  sleek solid-black feline; bright glowing YELLOW eyes with black slit pupils; white
  whiskers; no collar, no markings, no visible pattern. Reads LARGE against the furniture
  (big-cat / panther scale) — one pass called it domestic-sized, so scale is the one
  uncertain attribute. Confirm on the frames.
  NOTE: colour here is VLM-reported, which outranks any typed note (Alex is colour blind).
