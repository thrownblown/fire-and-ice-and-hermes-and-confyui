## Lineage note
Frank Frazetta's 'Death Dealer' (horned-helm rider silhouetted against sky, raised axe) is the stylistic ancestor of Darkwolf. Fire & Ice quotes it directly in shot_00029 (rider silhouetted on a mountaintop, backlit, arm raised). The Frazetta paintings are INSPIRATION-tier: they establish silhouette, posture, and menace, but are NOT the literal design and must be pruned from wired <Picture N> clip refs, or H3 drifts toward an armored knight on horseback (cuirass, eagle emblem, mount). The canonical design is the wolf-head hood worn by a human, per the production model sheet and the user's traits.
