# Darkwolf — Character Spec

**Name & Hook:** Darkwolf — the Lone Warrior, a mysterious barbarian ranger who walks out of the frozen wilds to be the thing the predators fear.

## Visual Design

**Silhouette & build:** A towering, oiled-muscle barbarian in the Frank Frazetta mold — broad shoulders, corded arms, heavy brow, a jaw like a trap. The dominant shape is the wolf-skin cloak: a pelt mantle whose head rides one shoulder like a second set of eyes, flaring wide behind him so his bare chest catches all the firelight. Resting pose is a coiled predator's crouch, weight forward, sword low, every frame a single-figure icon of mud-and-fire light against the snow.

**Hair & face:** Long dark wind-blown hair that catches a cyan neon rim on one edge, a short beard, a weathered scarred face, and a stare that never blinks — hard stone cut with ice.

**Costume layers (practical fur-and-stud fantasy):** A hooded wolf-skin cloak, the pelt's head mounted on one shoulder; bare chest and oiled muscle crossed by two studded-leather baldrics that strap the blades across his back in an X; studded leather bracers on the forearms; a single spiked pauldron on the shoulder opposite the wolf's head; a fur loincloth over a studded leather belt; fur-wrapped boots. No polished plate, no ornament — only hide, studs, chrome buckles, and honed steel.

**Signature props/weapons:** Twin blades — a plain long sword and a hunting knife, honed steel, no name, no decoration — plus a small wolf-skull totem on a leather thong at the throat (bone, not ornament).

**Color palette (80s hallmarks):** airbrushed gradients, chrome steel, and neon magenta/cyan/amber accents over a heavy-metal-album-cover base of practical fur-and-stud fantasy. No muddy brown — saturated purple-black plus chrome and neon.

## Backstory

A nameless wanderer of the ice wastes, Darkwolf steps out of the storm only when the world's heroes are already bleeding. He speaks little and asks nothing, but he fights with the certainty of a man who has already lost everything worth keeping. He is the wild made flesh — a wolf that walks upright, bound by no king and no law but the hunt. He respects strength and despises cruelty, so when the weak are hunted he becomes the thing their predators fear. In the end it is Darkwolf who closes the last distance to the sorcerer-king's reign of ice and iron and ends it, then turns back into the snow without a word — leaving tracks that vanish with the dawn.

## Personality & Voice

- Stoic and near-silent; speaks in short, final sentences.
- Patient as a predator, utterly fearless.
- Loyal only to the fight itself — no vanity, no ambition, no fear of death.
- Respects strength, despises cruelty; sides always with the hunted.
- Cannot stay: he protects, then disappears.

**Sample line:** "The wolf does not howl before it strikes."

## Abilities & Kit

- **Wolf-skin cloak** — a heavy pelt hood and mantle that lets him vanish into snow and shadow; the wolf's head rides his shoulder like a second set of eyes.
- **Twin blades** — a long sword and a hunting knife, plain steel, honed to a killing edge; no ornament, no name.
- **Master tracker** — reads spoor, blood, and broken branches across miles of ice; nothing that flees him stays hidden.
- **Uncanny strength** — a Frazetta frame of oiled muscle that moves like a storm; he fights bare-chested in the killing cold and never slows.
