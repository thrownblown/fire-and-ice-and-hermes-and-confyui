# Darkwolf — Character Sheet

**Name & Archetype:** Darkwolf — the Lone Warrior (mysterious barbarian ranger of the frozen wilds)

## Backstory
A nameless wanderer of the ice wastes, Darkwolf appears from the storm when the world's heroes are already bleeding. He speaks little and asks nothing, but he fights with the certainty of a man who has already lost everything worth keeping. When Larn and Teegra are hunted by Nekron's sub-human horde, Darkwolf is the shadow that keeps them alive — tracking the enemy through blizzard and black rock, killing without ceremony, and vanishing before thanks can be offered. He is the wild made flesh: a wolf that walks upright, bound by no king and no law but the hunt. In the end it is Darkwolf who closes the distance to Nekron and ends the sorcerer-king's reign, then turns back to the snow without a word.

## Personality
Stoic and near-silent; speaks in short, final sentences. Patient as a predator, utterly fearless, and loyal only to the fight itself. He respects strength and despises cruelty. No vanity, no ambition, no fear of death — only the quiet certainty of a man who has made peace with the wild.

**Motivation:** The hunt. He fights because the strong prey on the weak, and he has chosen to be the thing the predators fear.
**Flaw:** He cannot stay. He protects, then disappears — leaving those he saved to wonder if he was ever real.
**Catchphrase:** (rarely spoken) "The wolf does not howl before it strikes."

## Signature Abilities & Items
- **Wolf-skin cloak** — a heavy pelt hood and mantle that lets him vanish into snow and shadow; the wolf's head rides his shoulder like a second set of eyes.
- **Twin blades** — a long sword and a hunting knife, plain steel, honed to a killing edge; no ornament, no name.
- **Master tracker** — reads spoor, blood, and broken branches across miles of ice; nothing that flees him stays hidden.
- **Wolf-skull totem** — a small wolf skull strung on a leather thong at the throat; bone, not ornament — his one talisman to the wild.
- **Uncanny strength** — a Frazetta frame of oiled muscle that moves like a storm; he fights bare-chested in the killing cold and never slows.

## Visual Description
A towering, oiled-muscle barbarian in the Frazetta mold — broad shoulders, corded arms, a body built for the kill. He wears a wolf-skin cloak, the pelt's head draped over his shoulder and the fur framing a face of hard, weathered stone: deep-set eyes, a heavy brow, a jaw like a trap. Bare chest and arms, a fur loincloth and studded leather belt, fur-wrapped boots. Long dark hair whipped by the wind, a short beard, and a stare that never blinks. He carries a long sword and a hunting knife, plain steel, and moves with the coiled silence of a predator — every frame a single-figure icon of mud-and-fire light against the snow.
