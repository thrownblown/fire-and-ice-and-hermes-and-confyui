# Fire & Ice — Character Physical Descriptions (DRAFT for curation)

Physical-appearance attributes used to map the VLM's observations back to
character identity. The VLM was not asked to name anyone: free-form naming
invented characters and merged distinct people. Identity comes from matching
these descriptions against the pass, by hand or by a later rule. A description
that fits nobody stays unnamed.

## Leads

- **Larn** — young adult man; athletic/muscular; shoulder-length blond hair;
  brown loincloth with a gold buckle, arm bands, black boots; often
  bare-chested; carries a sword. *(Hair confirmed blond on source stills
  shot:00873 — not dark.)*
- **Teegra** — young adult woman; pale skin; very long dark hair; slender;
  light purple two-piece (sleeveless top and skirt), not a fur bikini.
  The fur-bikini line was wrong.
- **Darkwolf** (credited as Rescuer, scene 8) — older warrior; very large, heavily
  muscled; dark/weathered skin; long black hair (often tied back); bare-chested
  with a loincloth and wrist/ankle bands; carries a sword/axe.
- **Nekron** — tall, gaunt/pale young man; sharp features; long hair that
  reads white in the VLM pass (design notes say black); dark robes; staff.
