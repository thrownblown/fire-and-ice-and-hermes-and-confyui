## JULIANA

Pale, almost white skin. Angular face, sharp jaw, pointed chin. Heavy dark eyeshadow, thin arched black brows, crimson lipstick. Very long straight jet-black hair, pulled back at the crown with a row of small white beads. Long flat silver drop earrings, dagger- or teardrop-shaped. Deep purple long-sleeved gown, plunging V-neck, hip-high slits exposing bare legs.

Not Teegra. Teegra is a light purple two-piece and gold hoop earrings. Juliana is a full gown, silver dagger earrings, beads at the crown, red lipstick.

Refs:

![](corpus/refs/juliana_portrait.jpeg)
![](corpus/refs/juliana_chains.jpeg)

Prompt line:

A 1980s dark fantasy villainess with extremely pale, stark white skin and harsh, angular facial features. She has long, straight jet-black hair with small white beads at the crown. She wears heavy dark eyeshadow, thin arched eyebrows, and stark red lipstick. She is wearing long, silver dagger-shaped drop earrings and a solid deep-purple, long-sleeved gown with a deep plunging V-neck. The gown's skirt has extreme hip-high slits on both sides, exposing her bare pale legs. Ralph Bakshi and Frank Frazetta rotoscoped animation style.

## LARN

Blond, muscular, brown belt with a gold buckle, bone necklace.

More stills in `corpus/larn/`: a ledge with Darkwolf, an embrace with Teegra, and an underwater rage frame. The embrace and the ledge include other people, so crop before using them as a sole ref. The embrace and the underwater frame carry a watermark. Do not feed those to H3 until it is cropped off.

## TEEGRA

Long black hair, light purple two-piece, gold earrings. One later still shows her in a sheer robe and veil among subhuman captors: `corpus/teegra/teegra_sheer_robe_captors.jpeg`. That frame has other figures in it, so it is not a solo ref.

## ROLIEL

Older than Teegra. Pale skin, long red hair, amber eyes. Flowing white robe, blue braided belt, blue choker, blue wrist cuffs. Reads as a witch, not a girl in a two-piece.

Source stills: `corpus/roliel/` (20 images). Descriptor taken from one of them by the local vision model. Alex should confirm before it is used as a reference.

## DARKWOLF

Muscular, bare-chested, long black hair. Black wolf mask with pointed ears and yellow eyes. Bone-and-skull necklace. Large double-bladed axe. Fur loincloth, tall boots. Not a blonde man and not a woman.

Stills: `corpus/darkwolf/` (6 character frames plus the color model sheet).

The mounted Frazetta paintings are not his face. They are the silhouette used for one early shot of him on a mountain ridge, as foreshadowing. Do not use them as an identity ref.

## JAROL

Older, bald, long white beard and mustache. Red or maroon tunic, gold hoop earrings, a necklace of large gold oval medallions. Not Nekron: Nekron has long white hair and a dark robe. Jarol is bald.

Stills: `corpus/jarol/` (4). The close-up and the shouting frame are the cleanest identity refs. The other two include Teegra and Darkwolf, so crop before using them as a sole ref.

![](corpus/jarol/jarol_closeup.webp)

## OTWA

Huge, muscular, long reddish-brown hair, weathered face, heavy-lidded eyes. Tan loincloth. Far larger than the other men. Not Larn: Larn is blond, with a brown belt and a bone necklace.

Stills: `corpus/otwa/` (2).

![](corpus/otwa/otwa_closeup.jpeg)

## TARO

Young man, long straight black hair, thick dark brows. Red vest or tunic, gold armbands, sometimes a sword. Not Nekron and not Darkwolf: no white hair, no wolf mask, no axe. Not Jarol: Jarol is bald with a white beard.

Stills: `corpus/taro/` (5). The close-up is the clean identity ref. The tent frame and the mounted rally include other warriors. The sword frame has a fallen figure in the corner. Crop those before using them as a sole ref.

![](corpus/taro/taro_closeup.webp)

## ICE WITCH

Dark-skinned woman, large afro, white face paint, gold hoop earrings, reddish-brown outfit. Roars among ice and skulls. Not Juliana: Juliana is pale, with straight black hair and a purple gown. Not Roliel: Roliel has red hair and a white robe.

One still: `corpus/ice-witch/ice_witch_roar_skulls.jpeg`.
