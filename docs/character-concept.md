# Character Concept — Darkwolf

**Name:** Darkwolf
**Archetype:** Lone Warrior (mysterious barbarian ranger of the frozen wilds)

## Backstory

A nameless wanderer of the ice wastes, Darkwolf steps out of the storm only when the
world's heroes are already bleeding. Beneath the aurora-lit peaks where Nekron's glacial
sorcery has fused with humming chrome and neon sigil-light, he is the wild made flesh — a
wolf that walks upright, bound by no king and no law but the hunt.

He tracks Larn and Teegra's pursuers across blizzard and black rock where a laser-grid of
dead sorcerer-engines still pulses beneath the snow, killing without ceremony and speaking
only in short, final sentences. He respects strength and despises cruelty, so when the weak
are hunted he becomes the thing their predators fear. He has already lost everything worth
keeping, and fights with the certainty of a man who has made peace with the wild.

In the end it is Darkwolf who closes the last distance to the sorcerer-king's reign of ice
and iron, and ends it — then turns back into the snow without a word, leaving tracks that
vanish with the dawn and a legend that half-frozen travelers still argue over around the
campfire.

## Personality

- Stoic and near-silent; speaks in short, final sentences
- Patient as a predator, utterly fearless
- Loyal only to the fight itself — no vanity, no ambition, no fear of death
- Respects strength, despises cruelty; sides always with the hunted
- Quietly protective of the weak he lets travel beside him, though he never says so

**Motivation:** The hunt. He fights because the strong prey on the weak, and he has chosen
to be the thing the predators fear.

**Flaw:** He cannot stay. He protects, then disappears — leaving those he saved to wonder if
he was ever real at all.

**Catchphrase:** (rarely spoken) "The wolf does not howl before it strikes."

## Signature Abilities & Items

1. **Wolf-skin cloak** — a heavy pelt hood and mantle that lets him vanish into snow and
   shadow; the wolf's head rides his shoulder like a second set of eyes.
2. **Twin blades** — a long sword and a hunting knife, plain steel honed to a killing edge;
   no ornament, no name.
3. **Master tracker** — reads spoor, blood, and broken branches across miles of ice; nothing
   that flees him stays hidden.

## Design Notes (for downstream)

Blend: a Frank Frazetta brute hardened by 80s commercial fantasy — bare oiled muscle and
wolf-pelt fur against deep-purple storm night, fire-orange torch glow, and a cyan neon rim
light on snow and steel. The laser-grid floor under the traditional duel is the era's tell.
Dominant palette drawn from the aesthetic brief: deep-purple `#2D0A4D` night, chrome
`#C0C8D8` steel, fire-orange `#FF6A00` and gold `#FFC300` warmth, blood-red `#B3001B` for
the kill, neon-cyan `#00F0FF` as the cold 80s rim.
