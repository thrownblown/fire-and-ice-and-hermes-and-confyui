# Visual Spec — Darkwolf (Lone Warrior)

Character: **Darkwolf** · Archetype: Lone Warrior (barbarian ranger of the frozen wilds)
Basis: `character-concept.md` (t_632e85ca) + `aesthetic-brief.md` (t_9038a8f5)

## Silhouette, Build & Pose-Read

A hulking, long-limbed barbarian ranger — broad bare shoulders and an oiled, scarred
torso under a heavy wolf-pelt mantle, so the top of his body reads as a single dark,
shaggy mass broken only by the wolf's head riding one shoulder. The silhouette is
top-heavy and fur-broken, tapering through a fur loincloth to wrapped calves and boots,
exactly the "wolf walking upright" the concept demands. His default pose is a predator's
stillness — weight low and forward, knees softly bent, chin tucked, hands resting near the
twin blades — coiled to spring rather than posed to flex; even at rest he reads as about to
move.

## Costume (piece by piece)

**Head — wolf-pelt hood & mantle.** A hooded wolfskin drape, the pelt's head mounted on the
right shoulder like a second set of eyes. The fur is deep-purple-black shadow rather than
brown (no muddy medieval leather — the brief bans it); highlights are chrome `#C0C8D8`
picked out along the guard hairs and a cyan `#00F0FF` rim where the aurora catches the pelt.
The hood frames a weathered, unreadable face.

**Torso — bare, crossed baldrics.** Bare oiled muscle with visible scars, oiled to a
Frazetta sheen. Two crossed studded-leather baldrics strap the blades across his back; the
studs are chrome, and the crossing forms an X that breaks the silhouette. No shirt — the
bare chest is the era's tell, but the crossed straps and fur give him mass instead of
nudity.

**Arms — studded leather bracers.** Forearm-to-elbow bracers of dark leather set with chrome
studs and a single small chrome buckle each; the hands stay bare for grip. A thin band of
fur at the elbow repeats the pelt. One shoulder carries a small spiked pauldron (embraced
impractical-spiked-armor cliché) to counterbalance the wolf's head on the other side.

**Legs — fur loincloth over leather.** A fur-trimmed loincloth (wolf pelt again) over dark
leather leggings, laced at the sides with chrome eyelets. A studded belt hangs low on the
hips; a hunting knife in a plain leather sheath sits at the small of the back.

**Footwear — fur-wrapped boots.** Tall wrapped boots of hide and fur, laced with leather
thongs and a chrome buckle at the ankle; the fur wrap breaks the ankle line and muffles
footfall, matching the tracker's silence.

## Hair, Face, Makeup & Markings

Hair long, black and wind-blown — feathered and unkempt, framing the face and catching the
neon rim light in cyan on one edge. A short dark beard and heavy brow deepen the stoic,
near-silent read. Face weathered and scarred (one old blade-scar across the cheek); no
cosmetic makeup. Eyes are the only bright point: a faint cyan `#00F0FF` glint in otherwise
dark eyes, so in shadow he reads as eyes-first. Optional weather marking: a single streak
of blood-red `#B3001B` war paint down one cheekbone for the kill — kept minimal, never
tribal pastiche.

## Signature Props / Weapons

1. **Wolf-skin cloak** — the hooded pelt mantle itself; lets him vanish into snow and
   shadow, the wolf's head always at his shoulder.
2. **Long sword** — a plain, unornamented long sword in a back sheath; steel honed to a
   killing edge, no name, no decoration, chrome `#C0C8D8` highlight along the fuller.
3. **Hunting knife** — a short plain-steel blade at the small of the back, twin to the
   sword's plainness; his silent finisher.
4. **Wolf-skull totem** — a small wolf skull or tooth strung on a leather thong at the
   throat, the one personal talisman; it nods to the tracker's bond with the wild without
   contradicting the concept's "no ornament" rule (it is bone, not decoration).

## Color Palette

Drawn directly from the aesthetic brief's 8 swatches; dominant first, then accents.

| Hex | Role |
|-----|------|
| `#2D0A4D` | **Dominant** — deep-purple night: his fur, shadow, and base |
| `#C0C8D8` | Chrome steel — blades, studs, buckles, baldrics |
| `#00F0FF` | Neon cyan — cold aurora rim light on fur, steel, eyes |
| `#FF6A00` | Fire-orange — torch/warmth gradient on skin and steel |
| `#FFC300` | Sunset gold — highlight on oiled muscle and buckles |
| `#B3001B` | Blood red — the kill, war paint, danger accent |

Dominant is `#2D0A4D` (the deep-purple base that grounds every neon); `#C0C8D8` and
`#00F0FF` are the primary functional accents, with `#FF6A00` / `#FFC300` for warmth and
`#B3001B` reserved for the kill. No brown appears anywhere — the look is saturated
purple-black plus chrome and neon, never mud.
