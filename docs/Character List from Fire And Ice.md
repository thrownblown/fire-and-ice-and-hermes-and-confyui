Response: Based on the script provided from "Fire And Ice," here is the list of characters:

### **Primary Characters**

> * **Nekron**: The Ice Lord and son of Juliana. He was tutored in the black arts and powers of the mind, and pushes a giant glacier southward to conquer the world.  
> * **Juliana**: Nekron's mother. She gathered an army, tutored Nekron, and seized control of the region of Ice.  
> * **King Jarol**: The generous king who rules the volcanic mountain region from his fortress, Fire Keep.  
> * **Princess Teegra**: King Jarol's daughter. She is kidnapped by Nekron's minions and hunted throughout the script.  
> * **Prince Taro**: King Jarol's son and Teegra's brother. He travels to Ice Peak as an envoy to negotiate with Nekron.  
> * **Larn**: A young survivor who fights against the Ice Lord and attempts to help Teegra.  
> * **Roliel**: A woman living in the woods who initially offers Teegra shelter, food, and drink. She later plots to sell Teegra to Nekron's dogs for a price.  
> * **Otwa (Great Otwa)**: Roliel's son. He is described by his mother as gentle, but he possesses immense strength and can twist a chain apart with his bare hands.  
> * **Shaitan**: A companion spoken to by Teegra when she complains about being shut up during the war.

### **Groups and Minor Factions**

> * **Subhumans / Nekron's dogs**: The minions serving Nekron who are responsible for kidnapping Teegra.  
> * **Dragonhawk Riders**: Forces commanded by King Jarol at Fire Keep.
