# Character Identities — Fire & Ice remade as a Technicolor 80s Sword-and-Sandals Epic

> *"halfway between Ben Hur and Beastmaster with an unlimited effects budget"*
> — Alex, 2026-10-13

Source: the 1983 Bakshi/Frazetta rotoscope cartoon. Target: a live-action
technicolor 80s sword-and-sandals epic — saturated colour, practical costumes
and armour (leather, brass, fur, hand-hammered metal), broad theatrical
performance, heroic low angles, epic practical scale. The content stays
Bakshi/Frazetta; the medium register changes to the live-action genre cinema
of the era.

Each identity below maps a cartoon character to their remake version:
what they are, what they look like through the house-style lens, what makes
them unmistakable on screen, and how they perform. The VLM pass profiles in
`library/char/profiles/` are observational ledger data from the source stills
— useful raw material, but they describe the cel animated figures, not the
live-action targets. These identities are the target.

---

## DARKWOLF — the antagonist

**Who he is.** The heavy. A mercenary warrior of the old schools — the kind
of man who has fought in a dozen kingdoms and sold his sword to most of them.
He is not a werewolf, not a cursed thing: he is a human being who wears a
skinned wolf's head as a helmet, and the joke is that it works. The helmet
makes him look like a monster, and the people who fear him believe it. His
real face sits underneath — tanned, square-jawed, yellow-eyed — and only the
mouth and chin show through the cut-away snout. He is older than Larn,
heavier in the hand, and he carries himself like a man who has never lost a
fight he cared about.

**Look.** Technicolor barbarian register — Beastmaster oiled muscle meets
Ben-Hur epic costume. Skin is deep tanned bronze, not cel-flat brown.
Long black hair in two thick braids over the chest (the model sheet's braid
is the truth; the film stills drop it sometimes). The wolf-head helmet is the
signature piece: a real skin, ears and eyes intact and looking forward, snout
cut away to expose the wearer's mouth and chin. Yellow eyes of the wolf read
as the wearer's own yellow eyes looking through — the two align.

Wardrobe is practical, hand-built, leather-and-brass: a wide fur-lined war
girdle (not a corset — no boning, no lacing, just a heavy leather band with
fur on the top edge, sitting from lower ribcage to hip) with round bronze
plates stitched along it; a leather loincloth below with circular medallion
patches; fur-lined vambraces on both forearms; calf-high fur-lined moccasins.
A necklace of sharp teeth and a small skull pendant at the throat. No metal
chest plate — the torso is bare and oiled, which is the Beastmaster pole of
the register. Bronze battle axe, single blade, hand-forged look.

Colours: bronze skin, black hair, black-brown leather, warm bronze metal,
the wolf helmet matte grey-brown with yellow eye windows. The teal-cyan eye
glow that the film stills show is a lighting effect, not the design truth —
design truth is yellow (per `ref_tiers.json` pixel probe). On screen in the
remake, the wolf eyes can glow amber or teal as the scene needs; the helmet
reads either way.

**Signature.** A massive figure in a wolf-head helmet, bare oiled chest, wide
fur-lined belt with bronze plates, axe in hand, braids over the chest. The
Death Dealer silhouette (Frazetta) is the homage baseline — shot_00029 in the
source film quotes it directly as a rider silhouetted on a peak with arm
raised. That silhouette survives into the remake as Darkwolf on horseback,
black horse, ridge against sky.

**Performance.** Broad, heavy, deliberate. He does not rush. When he moves
it is with the economy of a man who knows exactly how much force he needs.
The theatrical register applies: his roars are full-cheeked and loud (the cut
away snout gives him a real mouth to roar through), his posture is
dominating, his presence fills a widescreen frame. He is the villain you
believe could kill the hero with one blow.

## LARN — the hero

**Who he is.** The young hero. Late teens to early twenties, the age where
muscle is real but the body is still learning its own strength. He is the
Ben-Hur pole of the cast: the guy who grows from skilled swordsman to
something larger over the course of the picture. Blond, tanned, bare-chested
with a loincloth and arm bands — the sword-and-sandals hero template, but
rendered in technicolor saturation, not 1959 vintage tan-and-white.

**Look.** Skin is sun-tanned bronze, not pinkish-cel. Hair is blond,
shoulder-length or longer, often in a single thick braid down the back (the
VLM pass fought over braid vs. loose; the design truth is braid — it reads
cleaner on a hero and matches the Frazetta lineage). Large gold hoop earrings
are his recurring accent — they catch light in technicolor and read as a
character marker. A brown leather belt with a gold buckle, a necklace with a
circular pendant and teeth/fang beads, arm bands, calf-high boots. Often
bare-chested. Carries a sword.

Colours: blond hair (saturated, not washed out), tanned bronze skin, gold
metal, brown leather, the red of his belt and arm bands providing the
saturated accent the technicolor register wants. Blue eyes (the VLM pass
fought blue vs. brown; design truth from source stills is blue — shot:00873
confirms).

**Signature.** A tanned young man with a blond braid, gold hoop earrings, a
belt with a gold buckle, bare chest, sword at the hip — moving with the
confident physicality of someone who knows he is the protagonist. Heroic low
angles, wide framing, broad gesture.

**Performance.** Energetic, optimistic, physical. He moves big — the Ben-Hur
pole needs a hero who can carry a crowd scene and a single hero shot with the
same body. His gestures are broad and clear. He is not a brooding anti-hero;
he is the sword-and-sandals lead, which means he is straight-backed, direct,
and visibly capable. The Beastmaster side gives him the oiled-muscle physical
register; the Ben-Hur side gives him the scale.

## TEEGRA — the female lead

**Who she is.** The woman at the centre of the story — not a prize, not a
damsel, but the person the hero is trying to reach and the villain is trying
to control. She is the most important re-imagining in the cast, because the
source film's cel design put her in a fur-bikini that the VLM pass
misflagged (the `character_descriptions.md` note says: *"light purple
two-piece (sleeveless top and skirt), not a fur bikini. The fur-bikini line
was wrong."*). In the remake she gets the full technicolor sword-and-sandals
treatment: a real wardrobe, real presence, real agency.

**Look.** Skin is brown (design truth, not the pinkish cel wash). Hair is
very long, straight, black, loose with bangs — a visual anchor the VLM pass
converged on strongly (87/97 frames agree on black, long, straight). Her
wardrobe is a light purple sleeveless top and skirt — not a bikini, not a
fur bikini — rendered as real fabric with weight and drape in the technicolor
register. Gold jewellery: a pendant necklace, earrings. The purple is
saturated, the gold catches light.

The Beastmaster pole gives her the physical register: she is not a decorative
figure. She moves, runs, fights when she has to. The Ben-Hur pole gives her
the scale: she is present in crowd scenes and solo shots with the same
confidence.

**Signature.** Long black hair, brown skin, saturated purple top and skirt,
gold pendant, present and physical in every frame she occupies. Her silhouette
is clean and recognisable — the long black hair is the marker.

**Performance.** Direct, capable, emotionally present. She is not a passive
figure in the frame — she has physical business, she moves, she reacts. The
80s genre register gives her a physical confidence that the cel design
withheld. Her performance is broad but not exaggerated — she is a real person
in a big movie.

## NEKRON — the sorcerer

**Who he is.** The villain on the other side of the story from Darkwolf — the
dark sorcerer, Nekron's counterpart in the evil camp. In the source he is the
pale, white-haired figure in dark purple robes who opens the film. In the
remake he is the Ben-Hur pole of the villain register: a big, theatrical,
costumed presence, not a CGI blob. His power is shown through production
design (robes, sets, lighting) and performance, not through invisible effects.

**Look.** Skin is pale grey-purple in the cel — in the remake this becomes a
real pale complexion under technicolor lighting, with the purple reading as
colour grading and costume, not skin. Hair is very long, straight, white,
loose with bangs — a strong visual marker. His robes are dark purple with
black trim, real fabric with weight, belted. The VLM pass consistently read
him as fully dressed (robes cover the whole figure). His face is sharp,
intense, narrow-eyed — the sorcerer look, but rendered as a real face under
lighting, not a cel flat.

Colours: white hair (saturated), dark purple robes, black trim, silver metal
accents. The overall register is darker than the hero side — he lives in the
shadow registers of the technicolor palette, which is how thecolour handles
villains (deep saturated darks, not grey mud).

**Signature.** Very long white hair, dark purple robes, pale face, narrow
intense eyes — a figure who reads as a sorcerer the moment he is on screen.
His silhouette is high and lean, robe flowing.

**Performance.** Theatrical, controlled, cold. He is the Ben-Hur villain
register: large presence, deliberate gesture, a man who commands rooms and
battlefields. His performance is not physical in the muscle sense — it is
command, presence, stillness that reads as power. The 80s genre framing gives
him heroic low angles and wide framing that makes him loom.

## JULIANA — Nekron's mother

**Who she is.** Nekron's mother, a dark sorceress in her own right. In the
source she is an older woman in dark sorceress robes. The VLM pass had limited
data on her (28 rows). In the remake she is the matriarch of the dark side —
the woman behind Nekron, with the same sorcerer register but an older, more
established presence.

**Look.** Long black hair, very long, straight, loose. Dark purple robes,
fully dressed, with silver metal accents. She has the sorceress look — tall,
slender, robe-covered — but older, more regal, more established than Nekron.
A headband with gems, earrings. Purple gown with a high slit (the VLM pass
flagged this). Red lips as a colour accent.

Colours: black hair, purple robes, silver metal, red lip accent, light skin.

**Signature.** Long black hair, purple gown, gem-adorned headband, red lips —
a sorceress matriarch in the dark-purple register of the colour palette.

**Performance.** Regal, commanding, older and more settled than Nekron. She is
not a young sorcerer finding her power — she is a woman who has had it for a
long time. Her performance is still, deliberate, authoritative.

## KING JAROL — king of Fire Keep

**Who he is.** The king, the ruler of the fire keep, the established order
that the story disrupts. In the source he is an older king with grey/white
hair and beard, regal robes. In the remake he is the Ben-Hur pole of the
royalty register: a big, imposing, costumed presence, a real king on a real
throne in a real built set, not a cel figure in a painted background.

**Look.** Bald head, long white beard (these are the VLM pass's strongest
convergences — bald head is the top memorable trait, white beard is stable).
Skin is light brown in neutral light (VLM consensus). His costume is a red
sleeveless tunic/garment with gold circular accents — gold armbands, a gold
circular necklace, gold hoop earrings. The red is saturated technicolor red,
the gold catches light. He is fully dressed, imposing, shirtless underneath
the tunic in the barbarian-register sense (the tunic is a regal garment, not
a suit).

Colours: white beard (saturated), red garment (saturated), gold metal,
light brown skin.

**Signature.** Bald head, long white beard, red sleeveless tunic with gold
circular accents, gold armbands, gold hoop earring — a king who reads as a
king the moment he is framed. The white beard is the marker.

**Performance.** Imposing, regal, fatherly but authoritative. He is the
established order — large presence, deliberate movement, the weight of a
king. The Ben-Hur register gives him the scale: he sits on a real throne in a
real built set (the technicolor interior string — torchlit stone halls, heavy
drapery, brass, theatrical painted sets). His performance is broad but
measured.

## PRINCE TARO — Jarol's son, Teegra's brother

**Who he is.** The prince, Teegra's brother, Jarol's son. In the source he is
a supporting character; the `character_descriptions.md` has him as TBD. In
the remake he is the younger royal — the prince who has to step up, the
brother figure, the secondary hero of the Fire Keep side. He gets the
sword-and-sandals prince treatment: less established than his father, more
physical than a court figure, in the same colour register as Fire Keep
(red/gold/light brown).

**Look.** (To be derived from source frames — he has limited VLM data.) The
assumption is: young adult male, tanned skin, red and gold costume elements
matching Fire Keep's palette, sword or spear as a weapon. The house style
treats him as a real young man in real costume, not a cel figure.

**Signature.** Fire Keep colours (red, gold), young andPhysical, sword/spear
— the prince of the fire keep, in the same technicolor register as his father
but younger and more mobile.

**Performance.** Energetic, capable, less settled than Jarol. He is the
younger man who has to grow into the role — which gives him a performance arc
(hesitation → confidence) that the broad physical register can carry.

## ROLIEL — the sorceress of the light side

**Who she is.** A sorceress on the light/hero side — long red hair, fair
skin, orange/red eyes, white flowing robe with a dark belt, blue teardrop
pendant necklace, matching earrings and bracelets. The `character_descriptions.md`
notes she is a Frazetta reference — target design, not film identity. In the
remake she is the magic figure on the hero side: the sorceress counterpart to
Nekron, but on the light register, with the same theatrical presence and
real-costume treatment.

**Look.** Long red hair — the VLM pass converged strongly on red, long, loose
(26/28 frames). Skin is light beige (24/28). Her robes are white flowing
garment with a dark belt — real fabric with weight. Blue accessories:
beaded choker with teardrop pendant, bracelets, earrings. Her eyes are
orange/gold/yellow — glowing in the source, which in the remake reads as
lighting on real eyes (the VLM pass was all over the eye colour; design truth
is orange-ish, and the glow is the magical register). Bare feet in some
frames (the VLM pass flagged barefoot as a memorable trait).

Colours: red hair (saturated), white robes, blue accessories, orange eyes,
light beige skin.

**Signature.** Long red hair, white flowing robes, blue beaded choker with
teardrop pendant, orange eyes — the light sorceress, in the white/blue
register that contrasts with Nekron's dark purple.

**Performance.** Ethereal but physical — she has the magical register, but
she is a real woman in real robes moving in real space, not a floating CGI
figure. The 80s genre framing gives her the theatrical presence: wide angles,
confident framing, real physical business. Her performance is calmer than the
hero register — she is the magical authority, not the sword-and-sandals lead.

## OTWA (Great Otwa) — Roliel's son

**Who he is.** Roliel's son, gentle but immensely strong. The VLM pass had
him as middle-aged, muscular, tall, red very long hair, loincloth,
partially clad — with a gaunt face, wrinkles, and a lot of anguish/open-mouth
business in the source frames. In the remake he is the gentle giant of the
hero side — the strong man who is not a warrior by nature, the physical
counterweight to the more aggressive figures.

**Look.** Long red hair (stable in the VLM pass — 8/8 frames), very long,
straight, loose with bangs. Skin is pale/light (6/8). Muscular build, tall.
His wardrobe is a simple brown loincloth — partially clad, bare chest. The
source frames show him with a gaunt, wrinkled face and a lot of open-mouth
anguish — in the remake this becomes a real face under lighting, with the
wrinkles and expression rendered as performance, not cel flatness. Yellow
loincloth accents in some frames.

Colours: red hair (saturated), pale skin, brown loincloth, yellow accents.

**Signature.** Long red hair, bald/gaunt face, muscular bare chest, simple
brown loincloth — the gentle giant, in the red-hair register that links him
to his mother Roliel.

**Performance.** Gentle, strong, physically substantial but not aggressive.
He is the guy who can lift anything but does not want to fight — which is a
performance register in itself (large body, restrained movement, anguish when
pushed). The broad physical register gives him the scale; his performance is
the restraint.

## SHAIITAN — Teegra's companion

**Who he is.** Teegra's companion at Fire Keep. The VLM pass had him as a
puzzling figure — the 8B kept reading him as a black panther/cat-like creature
with yellow eyes, whiskers, pointed ears, black fur. The `character_descriptions.md`
lists him as TBD. Two possibilities: he is a human companion who the 8B
misread because of costume/lighting, or he is genuinely a creature companion
(a Beastmaster-style animal companion, which fits the house style perfectly).

**Look (if creature).** Black fur, yellow eyes, pointed ears, whiskers — a
black panther or large cat companion, rendered as practical creature effects
on an unlimited budget (the direction doc's explicit register). This is the
Beastmaster pole of the cast in its literal form: a real animal companion,
practical effects, high-contrast lighting, vivid colour. The yellow eyes read
as eye shine under lighting.

**Look (if human).** A human companion misread by the 8B — would need source
frame review to establish. The assumption pending review is the creature
reading, because it fits the house style and the VLM pass converged on it
strongly (5/5 frames agree on yellow eyes, black fur, whiskers).

**Signature (creature).** A black panther with yellow eyes, moving through
scenes alongside Teegra — the animal companion, in the Beastmaster register.

**Performance (creature).** Animal performance — practical effects, real
movement, high-contrast presence. The unlimited budget gives him real creature
effects, not a rubber suit. He is a presence in the frame, not a prop.

## SPIRIT — the skeletal figure

**Who he is.** A skeletal/ghostly figure — the VLM pass read a skeletal
humanoid with long white hair, black dress, visible ribcage, skull-like face,
black eye sockets. This is a supernatural figure — a spirit, a skeleton, a
ghost — in the source. In the remake, this becomes practical skeleton/makeup
effects on an unlimited budget: a real skeleton costume or makeup, rendered in
the technicolor register with high contrast and saturated colour, not a
cheap Halloween skeleton.

**Look.** Long white hair (stable — 8/10 frames), pale/white skin (the VLM
pass read white skin and pale skin), black dress/garment, black beaded
necklace/collar, black eye sockets (the skull-face reading). The skeleton is
the memorable trait. In the remake: practical skeleton makeup or costume,
white hair, black garment, high-contrast lighting, deep blacks and saturated
accents. The bleached white of the skeleton against the technicolor palette is
a strong visual.

Colours: white hair, white/pale skeleton, black garment, black eye sockets,
dark metal accents.

**Signature.** A skeletal figure with long white hair, black garment, hollow
eyes — a spirit/skeleton in the high-contrast technicolor register.

**Performance.** Supernatural, still, eerie. He is not a physical performer in
the muscle sense — he is a presence, a skeleton, a ghost. The 80s genre
framing gives him the theatrical eerie register: wide angles, high contrast,
real practical skeleton effects that read as a big-budget supernatural figure,
not a costume piece.

## TUTOR — the guide/teacher figure

**Who he is.** A guide or teacher figure — the VLM pass had a young adult
female (5/7 frames), black hair, light blue draped garment/clothing, gold
accents (choker, belt with circular ornaments), dark brown eyes. The
`character_descriptions.md` does not list Tutor separately. In the remake,
Tutor is the guide figure — the person who gives the hero information, the
teacher, the mentor. The light blue and gold register is distinct from the
other characters and gives Tutor a visual identity.

**Look.** Black hair (stable — 7/7 frames), straight, covered style (5/7 —
hair is covered/held back, possibly a headband or drape). Light blue
draped garment with gold circular ornaments on the belt — real fabric, real
metal. Dark brown eyes. Brown skin (5/8 frames). The gold choker and circular
belt ornaments are the markers.

Colours: black hair, light blue garment, gold metal, brown skin, dark brown
eyes.

**Signature.** Black hair (covered/tied), light blue draped garment, gold
circular ornaments on the belt and choker — the guide/teacher, in the
light-blue-gold register.

**Performance.** Calm, measured, informative — the guide register. Not a
warrior, not a sorceress, but a person with knowledge and presence. The
performance is restrained, deliberate, the person in the scene who explains.

## SUBHUMANS — Nekron's ape-like humanoid troops

**Who they are.** The subhumans — ape-like humanoid creatures, hunched,
fur-covered, primitive weapons. The `character_descriptions.md` lists them as
a group taxonomy. In the remake, these are practical creature effects on an
unlimited budget: real creature makeup/suits, hunched physical performance,
fur textures rendered in the technicolor register, primitive weapons as
real props. They are the minions of the dark side, the ground-level threat.

**Look.** Hunched posture, fur-covered, ape-like humanoid. In the remake:
practical creature makeup, real fur textures, hunched physical performance,
high-contrast lighting, deep colours. The technicolor register gives them
saturated fur tones (not grey mud), deep blacks, and the high contrast that
makes them read as threatening creatures in a big movie. Primitive weapons —
clubs, spears, rough weapons — as real props.

Colours: dark fur (saturated dark browns/ blacks), varied skin tones under
the fur, primitive weapon materials (wood, rough metal).

**Signature.** Hunched, fur-covered, ape-like, primitive weapons — the
subhumans, in the practical creature effects register.

**Performance.** Hunched, animalistic, group movement. They move as a pack,
hunched, threatening. The broad physical register gives them the scale; the
creature effects give them the presence. They are the background threat that
the hero side faces in numbers.

**Open question:** individual tracks — the `character_descriptions.md` says
"Group taxonomy; individual tracks assigned later." The Subhuman Captain and
Subhuman Leader have VLM profiles (distinct individuals within the group).
They get the same practical creature treatment but with individuating features
— a leader with a distinct weapon, a captain with a distinct posture.

## DRAGONHAWK RIDERS — Jarol's mounted forces

**Who they are.** Jarol's mounted forces — warriors on dragonhawk mounts. In
the remake, these are the mounted cavalry of the fire keep, the Ben-Hur pole
of the cast in its literal form: riders on mounts, epic scale, widescreen
framing, broad physical performance. The dragonhawks are practical creature
effects (flying reptilian mounts — wings, scales, beak, the whole register).

**Look (riders).** Warriors in Fire Keep colours (red, gold, light brown),
sword-and-sandals register, mounted. Real costume, real armour, real weapons.
The riders are the human pole — sword-and-sandals warriors on horseback-like
mounts.

**Look (dragonhawks).** Flying reptilian mounts — practical creature effects,
scales, wings, beak, tail. The technicolor register gives them saturated scale
colours, deep blacks, high contrast. They are not cel dragons — they are
practical creatures on an unlimited budget.

**Signature.** Riders on dragonhawk mounts — the mounted forces, in the
Ben-Hur epic mounted register with practical creature effects.

**Performance.** Mounted, broad, epic — riders with broad physical performance
on practical creature mounts. The widescreen framing and heroic low angles
give them the Ben-Hur scale. The dragonhawks are real creatures moving in real
space.

  the VLM pass was all over eye colour; the house style treats glow as
  lighting, not necessarily a design truth.

  above assume the saturation lands; that assumption needs the probe result to
  confirm. Until the probe lands, treat the colour descriptions as target,
  not measured.
